Source: https://huggingface.co/udgamlabs/Vemana-Base
Pinned revision: `93cefe2594c5157e4aa8eb9908639c253652fed9`.

The publisher releases the weights/tokenizer under Apache 2.0 and provides a NOTICE
describing its training-data sources and their terms. The original LICENSE, NOTICE,
model card, configuration and file-checksum manifest are preserved under
`model_metadata/Vemana-Base/`. These materials are third-party content and retain
their original licenses. Actual model weights/tokenizer assets are downloaded
separately; they are not committed to this repository.

No pretrained Gemma weights were imported by this study. The Vemana publisher's
NOTICE states that its weights and tokenizer were trained from random initialization;
the architectural layout does not by itself establish a separate checkpoint license.
Consult the publisher's actual notices for the model you choose to use.
