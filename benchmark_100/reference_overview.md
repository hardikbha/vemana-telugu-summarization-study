# Reference overview — source-derived, written for comparison

This reference is **not a Qwen-generated summary** and is not substituted for the
model's results. It describes the synthetic dataset's known contents.

The 100 synthetic complaints concern interrupted drinking-water supply (20), delayed
old-age pensions (15), damaged roads (15), recurring electricity interruptions (12),
garbage or blocked drains (10), unavailable medicines at health centres (8), missing
ration supplies (7), incorrect land records (6), and unusable school toilets (6).
One additional complaint reports fallen electrical wires by a school gate, danger
to children, and a request for immediate repairs. It does not confirm injuries or
that the electrical supply has been isolated.

Residents request inspection, restoration of services or benefits, repairs, and
clear information on responsible departments and next steps. They also request
acknowledgement numbers, progress updates, and verification before cases are closed.
The notes do not establish technical causes or confirm that the problems have been
resolved. The uncommon electrical danger should remain visible even though common
water and pension complaints dominate the batch.

All category counts come from the synthetic source manifest. They are not model
classification results, inferred real-world prevalence, or a claim about actual
CM helpline complaints.
